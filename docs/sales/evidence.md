# Public evidence on what goes wrong (read by us, 2026-11-29)

## JTA survey of departing visitors (Nov 18, 2025 - Jan 13, 2026; 4,110 answers at five airports)
https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/news08_00039.html
- 43.7% had no trouble at all.
- Trouble items: few trash cans 17.2%, talking with staff 15.4%, crowds 12.9%, transport 11.3%, multilingual signs 10.9%, credit/debit cards 10.7%, immigration 10.3%.
- Restaurants are the top place for staff-communication trouble (38% of those cases) and for card trouble (32% of those cases; most common: card not accepted).
- So roughly 6% of all visitors report communication trouble in a restaurant, and roughly 3% report card trouble in a restaurant.
- 68% handled communication trouble with a translation tool.

## What this does to our simulation
- Our data says 41% of meal shops do not take credit cards, yet only ~3% of visitors report card trouble at restaurants. Travelers adapt (they carry cash). So "no credit cards" is information to print, not a pain to sell on.
- Felt pain is a minority experience. The product should sell on what you gain (eating at the good places with confidence), not on fear.
- The survey has no item for "turned away / missed last order / could not book". Our simulation is the only estimate for that, and it concentrates in the ¥4,000+ band (docs/sales/trip_sim2.md).

## Rules found when checking 8 upper-band shops on Tabelog (11/29) that Google Maps hours do not show
- Last order 2.5 hours before closing (kaiseki, dinner LO 19:00); seating windows only (French: LO 12:30 / 19:30).
- 10% service charge (three shops), only after 16:00 at one; otoshi ¥440 only with alcohol at another.
- Solo diners must book by phone; counter seats age 13+ with a course; no children under 3; all tables 2 hours; one drink per person.
- Weekly closures that differ by season (closed Sundays and holidays April-October).
