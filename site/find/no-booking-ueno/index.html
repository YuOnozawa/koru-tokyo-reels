Walk-in friendly: no booking needed near Ueno

8 places in Ueno, Okachimachi & Yushima where you can just walk in. Places that list no reservations needed or taken, so you can just show up. Checked Nov 2026; hours change, so check Google Maps on the day. PICK = Yu's own picks.

AkihabaraAsakusaKandaEast Tokyo
Plan my Tokyo meals free 🎫
  • Tachinomi Takioka 立飲み たきおか
    Ueno · Standing bar (izakaya)
    Hours Daily 7:00-23:30
    Closed None (open daily)
    Price lunch ¥1,000-3,000 / dinner ¥1,000-3,000
    Order Cheap drinks with small plates such as grilled offal
  • Ueno Seiyoken Main Restaurant 上野精養軒 本店レストラン
    Ueno · Yoshoku (Japanese-style Western)
    Hours Tue-Fri 11:00-17:00 (LO 16:00); Sat, Sun, holidays 10:30-18:00 (LO 17:00)
    Closed Monday
    Price lunch ¥2,000-3,000
    Order Hayashi rice or beef stew
  • Yokohama Ramen Asagaya Ueno 横濱ラーメン あさが家 上野店
    Ueno · Ramen (Yokohama iekei)
    Hours Daily 12:00-17:00, 18:00-3:00 (closed 17:00-18:00 for soup prep)
    Closed None (open year-round)
    Price lunch ¥1,000-2,000 / dinner ¥1,000-2,000
    Order Special ramen (marked as recommended by the shop)
  • Garyu Tantanmen Takeko Tenjinshita 我流担々麺 竹子 天神下店
    Yushima · Ramen (tantanmen)
    Hours Mon-Sat 11:00-5:30 (next morning); Sun & holidays 11:00-23:00
    Closed None (open daily)
    Price lunch under ¥1,000 / dinner under ¥1,000
    Order Tantanmen (sesame and chili noodle soup)
  • Maizuru (raisin bread and coffee) ぶどうパンとコーヒーの店 舞い鶴
    Yushima · Bakery (raisin bread) and coffee
    Hours Mon-Sat 10:00-18:00
    Closed Sunday and public holidays
    Price lunch under ¥1,000
    Order Budo-pan (raisin bread), the shop's namesake
  • Mendokoro Onuma 麺処 大沼
    Yushima · Ramen and tsukemen
    Hours 11:00-19:00
    Closed Monday
    Price lunch under ¥1,000 / dinner under ¥1,000
    Order Tsukemen (dipping noodles) or ramen
  • Menya Genzo 麺屋 げんぞう
    Yushima · Ramen (shio, miso, tantanmen)
    Hours Lunch Mon-Fri 11:45-14:00; dinner Mon-Sat 17:30-23:30
    Closed Sunday and public holidays
    Price lunch under ¥1,000 / dinner under ¥1,000
    Order Shio (salt) ramen or tantanmen
  • Yushima Ramen 湯島らーめん
    Yushima · Ramen (shoyu)
    Hours Mon-Fri 11:30-22:00
    Closed Saturday, Sunday and public holidays
    Price lunch under ¥1,000 / dinner under ¥1,000
    Order Yushima ramen (shoyu), the house namesake bowl
