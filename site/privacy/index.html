Privacy

Tokyo Food Ticket Machine is run by Yu Onozawa (Instagram @koru.tokyo_guide), an individual in Tokyo, Japan.

What the planner collects

Making a plan, the gacha, search and kept tickets work in your browser only. Kept tickets are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere.

If you ask me to email your plan, I store your email address, the country you chose (optional), your plan settings (dates, areas, budget, foods you avoid) and the plan itself.

Usage counts

The site counts a few anonymous events per day (for example "a plan was printed" or "My Trip was opened") to see what people use. No names, emails, cookies or device IDs are attached.

My Trip

Your plan, saved places, flight and hotel details, money records, checklist, phrases progress and allergy card are stored in your browser on your device only. "Share my trip" puts only your plan, saved places and (if you tick it) your dates into the link, never your flight, hotel or money. The currency rate button asks open.er-api.com for today's rate; nothing about you is sent.

Likes, photos and notes

When you tap Like, Want to go or Been here, only the total count for that restaurant is stored. Which buttons you pressed is saved on your device only.

If you send a photo or a note, I store it with the name you typed (optional) and the date. A person checks every photo and note before it appears on the site. Approved photos and notes are public on this site and may be shared on Instagram with the name you gave. Don't send photos that show other people's faces up close, or anything you didn't take yourself. DM me on Instagram to have yours removed.

How I use it

To send you the plan you asked for. If you ticked the box, also to send occasional Tokyo food tips. I may count requests in total (for example, how many people plan trips to Asakusa) to decide what to cover next. I never sell your email or share it with restaurants.

Where it is stored

In a private Google Sheet and Gmail account used only for this project (Google LLC processes the data on my behalf).

Your choices

Reply "unsubscribe" to any email, or DM me on Instagram, and I will stop emailing you and delete your data within 30 days.
